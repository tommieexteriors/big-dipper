    How Big Dipper decides

    1 Business quality

    8 fundamentals, 0 to 100: valuation (P/E, PEG), profitability (ROE, margin), growth (revenue, earnings) and financial health (debt, free cash flow). Is the company behind the drop still sound?

    2 Price setup

    4 signals, 0 to 100: RSI, Bollinger Bands, the 200-day trend and volume. Is the selling stretched and exhausted, with the long-term trend still intact?

    3 The gate

    A weak business can never be a Buy, however oversold the chart looks. A buy needs a combined score of 60+ and a price-setup score of 55+, the same entry rule we backtested.

    Position size and stop-loss come from the risk profile you pick: the size is set so that hitting the stop costs about the investor's risk budget per trade. Fundamentals are current figures from Yahoo Finance and are not backtested, because free data has no point-in-time history. Full detail is in the project's methodology notes.
